**Codebook & Survey Tidier**

📊Codebook & Survey Tidier is a lightweight, browser-based tool designed for researchers in the humanities and social sciences. It instantly transforms messy survey exports (from Google Forms, Yandex Forms, or MS Forms) into clean datasets, documented codebooks, and ready-to-run SPSS syntax.

🔒 **Privacy First:** Everything runs 100% client-side in your browser. No files or personal data are ever uploaded to an external server.

✨ **Key Features**
- Smart Header Standardization: Converts messy, long survey questions into clean, valid variable identifiers while preserving original wording as labels.
- Checkbox Question Splitting: Seamlessly splits "select all that apply" (multiple choice) questions into analysis-ready binary $0/1$ columns.
- SPSS Automation: Generates ready-to-run .sps syntax files with complete variable labels and value labels.
- Research-Ready Exports: Outputs a clean CSV (fully compatible with jamovi, SPSS, or R), a comprehensive Markdown codebook, and a standard Data Package JSON schema.
- Data Quality Checks: Flags potential issues like missing values, constant columns, or formatting inconsistencies.

🚀 **How to Use**
1. Open the tool in your browser (link to your GitHub Pages page).
2. Drop your raw survey export (.csv or .xlsx) into the upload zone.
3. Review the preview, adjust settings if needed, and download your ready-to-use package (clean data, codebook, and syntax).

🛠️ **Tech Stack & Philosophy**
Built for transparency and reproducibility in quantitative history and social research. Feel free to explore the source code, report issues, or contribute!
